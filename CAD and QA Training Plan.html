The 10-Day Execution Plan

Structured perfectly over two calendar weeks. Week 1 establishes foundations and automated routines. Week 2 tests those skills on broken drawings and transitions to sustained live project production.

📅 Week 1: Foundations, Cloudworx & Admin

Focus: Mastering PCL standards, deep Cloudworx video integration (AC03), and learning QA administrative routines.

Monday — Friday
Day 1 (Mon)
🎯 Goal: Familiarize with standards, deliverable types, and UNICLASS layers.
Theory (Stds)
Standards & Checklists

Review SF1/RL5 types and the fundamental CAD QA Checklist. Study AutoCAD Naming, UNICLASS layers, PCLayer routine, and Borders.

Practice
Layer 0 Correction

Identify deliverable types from 3 past projects. Receive a drawing with all linework on Layer 0. Run PCLayer, correctly assign lines to UNICLASS layers, and insert/edit a PCL Border.

Day 2 (Tue)
🎯 Goal: Practice handling point clouds to draft missing 2D elements.
Videos (AC03.01 & 02)
Cyclone UI & DBs

License Manager, DB Import, Toolbar, PC VPORTS, Point cloud colours, and Visibility.

Practice
Drafting Missing Lines

Receive a faulty drawing missing obvious linework. Connect license, load point cloud database, configure PC VPORTS, and draft missing walls/levels directly from the cloud.

Day 3 (Wed)
🎯 Goal: Practice advanced point cloud handling for complex 3D elements & UCS.
Videos (AC03.04-10)
UCS & Slicing Tools

Matching & Creating UCS. Vertical slices, Clipping Manager, and the View Linework Trick.

Practice
Complex Alignments

Master UCS manipulation to align with irregular buildings. Draft complex elements including a staircase and overhead structural linework using advanced slicing.

Day 4 (Thu)
🎯 Goal: Understand core QA principles, consistency, and 2D specifications.
Theory (QA Rev 6)
Principles & Specs

Study QA Guidelines Rev 6. Focus on Consistency, Accuracy (floor stacking), and Fit for Purpose. Review Grids, North Points, and the 2D Spec Checklist (p.21-28).

Practice
Full Setup Audit

From scratch: Load DB -> Align UCS -> Slice -> PCLayer -> Border. Perform an immediate QA audit on the created setup, strictly applying the Rev 6 guidelines.

Day 5 (Fri)
🎯 Goal: Master automated QA routines and project administration processes.
Theory (QA Admin)
Automated QA

Understand AutoCAD QA Routines, Project Archive procedures, and Record Sheet tracking.

Practice
Run & Record

Execute automated QA routines on a dataset, interpret the generated error logs, and accurately fill out a mock Record Sheet.

✅ Week 2: Mock Audits, Live Production & Sign-Off

Focus: Transitioning from theoretical checklists to identifying errors in seeded drawings, entering project meetings, and sustained live project production.

Monday — Friday
Day 6 (Mon)
🎯 Goal: Identify and correct planted errors in a controlled mock environment.
Intensive Practice
Seeded Mock Audit

Receive a drawing intentionally seeded with critical errors spanning Layers, Bad UCS/Slicing, missing linework, and invalid Border data.

Goal: Identify, document, and fix errors.

Day 7 (Tue)
🎯 Goal: Review mock audit performance and finalize drawing preparation.
Correction & Prep
Audit Review

Review the results of the Day 6 mock audit with a senior tech. Run the final automated QA routines on the corrected drawing and prepare mock sign-off paperwork.

Day 8 (Wed)
🎯 Goal: Understand project instructions and start live QA workflows.
Live Application
Meetings & Production

Attend a New Instruction Meeting to understand how projects are initially briefed.

Begin live project production. Work alongside a senior CAD technician to co-audit live drawing setups and borders.

Day 9 (Thu)
🎯 Goal: Engage in sustained live project production and Drafter feedback.
Live Application
Sustained Production

Continue live project production on the active QA queue.

  • Focus on UCS, slicing, and linework checks using AC03 methods.
  • Actively engage in Drafter communication and feedback loops.
🏅
Day 10 (Fri)
🎯 Goal: Execute an independent QA audit and achieve final sign-off.
Final Assessment
Independent QA

Perform an independent, complete QA audit on a small live project without direct assistance.

Complete project sign-off admin forms and formally present findings to the CAD Manager.
